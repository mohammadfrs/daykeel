# Daykeel

A private daily planner that keeps your day on course.

- **Morning, set your course:** one keel task, next-up tasks, recharge, gratitude, a bright spot.
- **Evening, log the day:** win, highlight, lesson, rating, habits.
- **Calendar, week, progress:** day scores, habit tracking, and whether your days move you towards your heading and values.

Open the app: https://mohammadfrs.github.io/daykeel/

Your entries are stored only in your own OneDrive (folder `Apps/Daykeel`) or in a folder you choose on your PC. This repository contains only the app code; no personal data is ever stored here.
